Our Story

From a Garage Idea to a Global Motion Engine

In 2021, three friends — an engineer, a designer and a dreamer — asked why turning an idea into a moving video was locked behind expensive tools and complex editing workflows. The answer should have been obvious: it shouldn't be.

We built ImagineAI to be the fastest, friendliest AI text-to-video generator on the planet. No tutorials. No editing skills. Just type the scene you imagine, and watch it come to life.

Today our community spans 150,000+ creators across 80 countries — filmmakers, marketers, game developers, students and grandparents discovering the magic of motion. And every single video starts the same way: with a few words.

Our Journey

The Road to 2 Million Videos

2021

The Spark

Three friends prototype the first text-to-video engine in a garage. A prompt moved for the first time — slow, buggy — and magical.

2022

Public Launch

ImagineAI goes live with 3 motion presets. 10,000 creators join in the first month. The waitlist breaks 50K.

2023

1 Million Videos

Our community creates its millionth video. We introduce commercial licenses and 2 new motion presets.

2024

Speed Revolution

The v2 engine turns any prompt into video in under 5 seconds. Mobile apps launch. Creators hit 150K.

2025

Global Motion Engine

2M+ videos, 80+ countries, API for developers — and the biggest motion library yet is coming.
